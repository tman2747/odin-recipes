Homemade Beef Stew

  • Prep Time: Approximately 20–30 minutes
  • Cook Time: 3 hours
  • Oven Temperature: 350°F

Ingredients

  • Stew beef
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • Cooking oil (for browning)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Potatoes (Simply Potatoes)
  • 1 can (29 oz) tomato sauce
  • 1 can Rotel diced tomatoes
  • Carrots
  • Peas
  • Onions
  • Water, as needed
  • 1 can beef broth (optional)
  • Kitchen Bouquet browning and seasoning sauce, to taste
  • Additional browning sauce, to taste (if using separately)

Instructions

1. Prepare the Beef

  1. Place the stew beef and ½ cup flour into a large bag.
  2. Seal the bag and shake until the beef is evenly coated in flour.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a skillet or oven-safe pot.
  4. Season the beef with salt and black pepper.
  5. Brown the beef in the hot oil on all sides.

2. Combine the Ingredients

  1. Transfer the browned beef to a large oven-safe pot, if not already using one.
  2. Add the chopped potatoes.
  3. Pour in the 29 oz can of tomato sauce.
  4. Add the can of Rotel tomatoes.
  5. Add the carrots, peas, and onions.
  6. Pour in water and, optionally, a can of beef broth until the pot is approximately halfway full.
  7. Add Kitchen Bouquet to taste.
  8. Add additional browning sauce if desired.
  9. Stir everything together.

3. Cook the Stew

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Place the stew in the oven and cook for 3 hours.
  3. Stir approximately every 45 minutes.
  4. Check the liquid level each time you stir, adding water as necessary to keep the stew from drying out.
  5. Continue cooking until the beef is tender and the vegetables are fully cooked.

Recipe Notes

  • Consistency: Adjust the water or broth depending on how thick you prefer your stew.
  • Seasoning: Taste and adjust salt, pepper, and browning sauce as needed.
  • Stirring: Check every 45 minutes to prevent sticking and make sure there is enough liquid.
  • Beef broth: Optional, but adds extra beef flavor.
