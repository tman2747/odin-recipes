Grandma's Noodles

Homemade egg noodles with mashed potatoes and creamy white sauce.

Ingredients

  • Egg noodles
  • Potatoes
  • ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  • Milk
  • Half-and-half
  • ½ stick butter for the cream sauce, plus additional butter for the mashed potatoes
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Instructions

1. Prepare the Potatoes

  1. Peel and chop the potatoes.
  2. Place them in a pot of water and bring to a boil.
  3. Cook until the potatoes are soft and tender.
  4. Drain the potatoes.
  5. Mash the potatoes, adding butter and a little half-and-half until smooth and creamy.
  6. Set aside.

2. Make the Cream Sauce

  1. Melt ½ stick of butter in a saucepan over low heat.
  2. Add ⅓ cup flour to the melted butter.
  3. Stir continuously with a whisk until the butter and flour are well combined.
  4. Gradually add half-and-half and milk while whisking to prevent lumps.
  5. Continue stirring until the sauce becomes smooth and thick.
  6. Adjust the consistency with additional milk as needed.
  7. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.

3. Cook the Egg Noodles

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil.
  2. Add the egg noodles.
  3. Cook until tender, following the package instructions.
  4. Drain the noodles.

4. Combine Everything

  1. Combine the cooked egg noodles with the prepared cream sauce.
  2. Stir until the noodles are evenly coated.
  3. Serve the creamy noodles with the mashed potatoes.

Grandma's Recipe Notes

  • Cream sauce: Use milk and half-and-half to adjust the richness and consistency.
  • Too thick?: Add a little more milk.
  • Too thin?: Continue cooking and stirring to thicken.
  • Mashed potatoes: Add butter and a small amount of half-and-half for a creamy texture.
  • Seasoning: Adjust salt and pepper to taste.
